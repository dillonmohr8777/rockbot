## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by Dillon: a local-first Next.js 16 application using React 19,
TypeScript, a small Node runtime for model adapters, filesystem-backed redacted
receipts, Vitest, and Playwright. The first release runs entirely on Dillon's
machine. A desktop wrapper may be added later without changing the product or
agent contracts.

## Users

- Dillon Mohr is the primary user. He operates as CEO and gives outcomes to one
  Marketing Chief command surface instead of coordinating individual bots.
- Codex acting as Marketing Chief is the governing orchestrator, final verifier,
  and only canonical queue writer.
- Claude, Grok, Codex, Ollama models, and future model providers are replaceable
  workers behind the same bounded agent and routine contracts.

## Product Purpose

Rockbot is a local, model-agnostic operating-team console. It turns a plain
request into an exact route, a bounded agent plan, visible execution, evidence,
approval state, and a decision-ready handoff. Success means Dillon can switch
the underlying model without changing how the business is governed, then see
what ran, what was proven, what remains blocked, and what needs his decision.

## Positioning

The model is replaceable; the operating contract is not. Rockbot combines the
calm, direct task UX of Grok Bot with Dillon's verified 54-routine curriculum,
specialist bench, heartbeat, maker-checker separation, and canonical authority
boundaries. Neighboring chat products cannot truthfully claim that exact
source-routed and evidence-sealed operating layer.

## Operating Context

Rockbot runs locally beside Dillon OS, the shared agent vault, canonical client
operations, authenticated model CLIs, and local models. Dillon searches or
selects an agent, enters a task, selects a model at the bottom of the sidebar,
chooses Observe or Workspace authority, and watches a normalized run transcript.
The inspector exposes the route, source freshness, routine, checks, risks,
approval state, and receipt without forcing him into a separate dashboard.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve one Marketing Chief interface and a flat specialist bench.
- Import all 54 recorded routines and the six cadence operators from the
  Rockbot Operating System knowledge pack.
- Expose the 15 native specialist definitions plus Marketing Chief.
- Route no more than three specialists concurrently and allow no more than two
  evaluator revision loops.
- Support Codex CLI, Claude Code, Grok Build, Ollama, and a deterministic demo
  adapter behind one streaming event contract; future adapters use the same
  interface.
- Default every real provider to Observe authority. Workspace edits require an
  explicit per-run choice and remain inside allowlisted local roots.
- Never use bypass-permission flags, place secrets in prompts or logs, read raw
  credential stores, or treat an installed provider as authenticated.
- Sending, posting, publishing, spend, deployment, account changes, destructive
  actions, and canonical queue writes remain approval-gated outside this app.
- Dillon OS and the shared agent vault are read authorities. Rockbot stores only
  local, redacted run receipts and never becomes a competing queue.
- The model selector remains anchored at the bottom of the sidebar.
- Exact states remain distinct: complete, partial, blocked, drafted, staged,
  deployed, sent, and verified are not interchangeable.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Rockbot. "Grok Bot" remains the source reference and historical
  alias, not a restriction on the selected model.
- The installed Grok Bot 0.16.0 shell is a binding topology and density
  reference: quiet light field, fixed conversation rail, compact system type,
  colored bot identities, large working canvas, and bottom composer.
- Protocol 54 contributes the evidence, verified, and approval-boundary state
  language. Its cobalt, green, and amber semantics may appear only where those
  meanings are real.
- The implementation and identity assets are original. Do not copy proprietary
  Grok Bot source or ship its private content.
- Voice is direct, specific, warm, and evidence-led. Avoid generic assistant
  language and inflated completion claims.

## Product Principles

1. One request becomes one visible, bounded operating run.
2. Keep the model replaceable and the governance invariant.
3. Put evidence before state and authority beside action.
4. Prefer a useful artifact and honest blocker over a fluent summary.
5. Make the common path quiet; make risk and human decisions impossible to miss.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic controls, screen-reader status
updates, 4.5:1 body-text contrast, reduced-motion support, touch-safe controls,
and useful desktop and narrow-mobile layouts are required.
